The Problem

Why Is Starting Tasks So Painful?

Traditional productivity apps worsen executive dysfunction through shame and overload

01. The Ambiguity Cliff

Large tasks ("Study for AI Midterm", "Write 10-page essay") lack an obvious 60-second entry point. The brain freezes in executive dysfunction.

02. The Doomscroll Trap

To numb task dread, people instinctively open social media or YouTube tabs. Dopamine algorithms capture focus for hours in trance-like doomscrolling.

03. Shame-Based Tools

Most productivity tools use red overdue alerts, penalty counters, and broken streak warnings—causing stress avoidance rather than momentum.

Solution Overview

What We Made: QuietStart

A calm, mobile-friendly web app built with zero build steps, zero frameworks, and zero shame

⚡ Quick Add & 🎙️ Voice

Type and press Enter, or dictate hands-free via Web Speech API. Zero forms or categories.

✨ Breakdown & 🤏 Unstick

AI splits tasks into 3–7 micro-steps, with an instant 'Make It Even Smaller' 30-sec unstick button.

▶ Just Start & 🌧️ Audio

Shows ONE step at a time with a 5-min timer and synthesized Brown Noise / Soft Rain masking.

🛡️ Anti-Doomscroll Shield

Detects when you drift away to other tabs for 3+ minutes and sends a kind browser reality check.

📅 Gentle Calendar & Repeat

Review quiet wins with calm dots. Repeat any successful past day's routine with one click.

🌙 Dark Theme & Rollover

Low-sensory midnight dark mode for night study, and unfinished tasks roll over with zero shame.

AI Architecture

Hybrid Generative + Symbolic Engine

Delivering 100% reliability through a graceful degradation pattern

1. Generative LLM Path (Online)

• Direct Browser API: Supports OpenAI (GPT-4o-mini) and Google Gemini (1.5 Flash).

• Cognitive Prompt: Constrains steps to 3–7 items, requires imperative action verbs, and enforces zero-guilt affective tone.

• Strict Privacy: Keys stay in browser localStorage only.

2. Symbolic Heuristic Fallback (Offline)

• Guaranteed Availability: Triggers if no key is entered, network drops, or API rate limits occur.

• Keyword Regex Classifier: Routes tasks into academic, chores, coding, admin, fitness, or meals.

• Template Synthesizer: Generates concrete action-verb steps with effortless entry points.

Behavioral Ergonomics

Stopping the Drift: Anti-Doomscroll Shield

Using the Page Visibility API to break algorithmic scrolling trances

Tab Drift Detection

Monitors document.visibilitychange. When the user switches to a feed for 3+ minutes, sends a gentle browser notification:

"Did an algorithm grab your attention? Take a breath. Come back whenever you are ready."

Compassionate Re-Anchoring

Returning to the tab plays a calming 528 Hz Web Audio sine wave and displays a zero-shame welcome toast reminding users to drop their shoulders and resume their 2-minute step.

Ethics & Roadmap

Ethics, Limitations & What Is Next

Responsible design practices and future technical directions

Ethical Guardrails & Limitations

• Self-Help Notice: Educational project, not medical/clinical advice.

• Client-Side Storage: Data stays in browser localStorage; zero tracking.

• Tab Dependency: Notifications require active browser session.

What Is Next

• WebGPU Local AI: Run quantized models (Gemini Nano) directly in browser memory.

• Spoken Step Guidance (TTS): Calming text-to-speech audio narration for steps.

• Adaptive Pacing: Auto-calibrate timers (2, 5, 10 min) to user energy.
